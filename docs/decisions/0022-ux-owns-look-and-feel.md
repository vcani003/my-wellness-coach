# 0022 — UX owns the look and feel; start calm

**Status:** Accepted · 2026-10-14 · Decided by Vero

## Decision
- The **UI/UX specialist** owns visual design as well as flows: **tone, mood, colour, type, spacing, iconography and motion.** "The tone and mood of the page plays a big role."
- **The starting mood is calm.** Unhurried, soft and reassuring, never busy, loud or nagging. This matches the voice (0020): warm, brief and practical.
- UX defines the design **tokens** (named colours, type, spacing and motion values). Frontend implements them and owns accessibility, so contrast must meet WCAG 2.2 AA (0002). Tokens are dark-mode-ready (0017).
- Vero picks the visual direction from options UX presents.
