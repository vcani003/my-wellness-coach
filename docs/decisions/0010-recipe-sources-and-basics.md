# 0010 — Where meals and recipes come from, and assumed basics

**Status:** Accepted · 2026-10-13 · Decided by Vero

## Decision
- **Meal ideas:** the AI suggests them from what's in the kitchen.
- **Recipes:** the user's **own recipes** come first. When the user hasn't provided one, the AI writes or interprets one.
- **Assumed basics:** the AI may assume common basics (like salt, oil and common spices) without the user listing them. When it relies on an assumed basic, it **reminds the user** and invites them to correct it if they don't have it.
