# 0032 — Item types and search

**Status:** Accepted · 2026-10-15 · Decided by Vero (CAT1, defaults).

- **See all:** a **By place | By type** toggle. **By place is the default**, and the choice is remembered per device.
- **The 12 types:** Produce · Meat & seafood · Dairy & eggs · Rice, noodles & grains · Sauces & condiments · Spices & seasonings · Canned & jarred · Baking · Snacks & sweets · Drinks · Leftovers · Other.
- Kitchen sorts new items itself (inferred). A correction the user states ("fish sauce goes under sauces") wins, and is remembered per kitchen.
- **Search** matches the name you used or the English name, accent-insensitive ("nuoc mam" finds "nước mắm").
