# 0039 — "I only have N" changes the recipe, not the kitchen

**Status:** Accepted · 2026-10-15 · Decided by Vero.

- When a recipe is open, amounts you mention ("I only have 2", "only 1 can, not 2") change **only the recipe**. The app may not know your full count, so "2" can mean "2 is what I'm using", not "2 is all I have".
- **The kitchen gets adjusted after cooking**, through the existing "Update your kitchen" step (0013), if you choose to. If you don't, the app infers what was used from the recipe, as it does today.
- Outside a recipe, "I have 2 chicken breasts" still updates the kitchen.
