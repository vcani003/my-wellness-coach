# 0009 — Meal ideas show name, why, and ingredients

**Status:** Accepted · 2026-10-13 · Decided by Vero

## Decision
Each meal idea shows:
- the meal name
- a short reason it fits (for example, "uses your spinach")
- which of the user's ingredients it uses, and anything missing

The **idea card** stays this light. Once a meal is chosen, a recipe can be shown; see [0010](0010-recipe-sources-and-basics.md) and OPEN-QUESTIONS M1. External recipe sites and ranking stay out of scope (spec §10).
