# Product Vision — Wellness Coach

> **Stub.** Vero will provide the full vision later. This is **context, not current scope**. Nothing here authorizes building beyond the Kitchen-Aware Assistant MVP ([epic](epics/kitchen-assistant-mvp.md)).

## What we know so far (from the MVP spec)
- **Wellness Coach** is a broader personal food and wellness product built around **specialist assistants**.
- **Kitchen-Aware Assistant:** the first slice and the current MVP.
- **Future assistants** mentioned: Health & Wellness, Fitness Coach, nutrition-related features, and possibly other personal assistants.
- **Shared user context** (identity, preferences, goals, event history, provenance) should be usable across assistants through defined interfaces, not through another assistant's prompt history.
- **Explicitly later:** Health and Fitness Coach UIs, calorie and workout tracking, receipt scanning, photo inventory, Siri, advanced freshness prediction, grocery purchasing, recipe-source ranking, and long-term behavioral prediction.
